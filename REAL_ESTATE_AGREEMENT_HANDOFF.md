# Real-estate agreement handoff

Status: draft business instructions for professional legal review. This is not legal advice and is not itself the client agreement.

## Website workflow

The public website collects a booking request only. It must not collect payment or a final-contract signature. After Ben reviews the request, the final agreement must be prepared for the correct client type and jurisdiction.

1. Confirm whether the client is a consumer/homeowner or a business client such as a realtor or brokerage.
2. Confirm the client's province and provide the applicable Ontario or Quebec agreement.
3. For a Quebec standard-form agreement, provide the applicable French version before asking the client to choose to be bound only by English.
4. Confirm the exact package, deliverables, add-ons, travel, taxes, total, payment method and due dates.
5. Send the final agreement for review and correction.
6. Obtain both parties' acceptance or signatures and preserve the exact version, timestamps and evidence of delivery.
7. Immediately send both parties a printable copy of the completed agreement.
8. Request only a payment method and amount permitted for that client and jurisdiction.

## Default real-estate service standards

- Edited listing photos and the basic 2D floor plan: targeted within 2 business days after the shoot.
- Walkthrough and drone video edits: targeted within 3 business days after the shoot.
- 360° virtual tour: targeted within 3 business days after successful capture.
- Delivery: private link; high-resolution and MLS/web-ready JPEG photos; PDF and JPEG floor plan; MP4 video; hosted 360° link.
- Revisions: one round of reasonable minor corrections requested within 5 calendar days.
- Excluded from the included revision: reshoots, changed staging, item removal, virtual renovation, new creative direction, or work outside the confirmed scope.
- RAW, unedited and project-source files are not delivered.
- Every real-estate package includes 50 final listing images total for properties up to 2,500 sq. ft. That total includes interior, exterior and drone photos plus one basic 2D floor plan; the floor plan is counted as one of the 50 images.
- Delivered photos, videos and floor plans are retained for 90 days as a courtesy, without a guarantee after that period.
- Premium 360 includes 90 days of hosting from delivery. Additional 90-day periods are CA$25 when requested before expiry.
- The client must download and back up delivered files.
- The first 50 km of round-trip travel is included. Additional round-trip travel is CA$0.70/km. Parking, tolls and special access costs must be disclosed and approved before confirmation.
- Standard real-estate requests have no retainer. Payment is due before unwatermarked, full-resolution delivery. Approved brokerage accounts may receive Net 7 terms.

## Default listing-media licence

Copyright remains with Fusco Media. After full payment, the named client, named listing brokerage, property seller, applicable MLS and ordinary listing-syndication services receive a non-exclusive licence to use the delivered media only to market the photographed property during the current listing.

The standard licence ends when the property is sold, withdrawn, the listing expires, or the original agent or brokerage no longer holds the listing. Re-listing or use by another agent, brokerage, builder, stager, architect, publication, advertiser or unrelated third party requires written permission or a separate licence.

Portfolio and social-media use by Fusco Media is a separate optional client choice. It is not a condition of service and should not be buried inside the listing licence.

## Drone instructions

The current aircraft is a DJI Mini 4 Pro configured with the standard battery at an operating weight below 250 g. Aerial work remains conditional on weather, visibility, airspace, privacy, property access, equipment configuration, safety and Transport Canada requirements.

The final agreement must not guarantee aerial coverage. When a safe and lawful flight is unavailable, the agreement should specify whether the aerial portion will be rescheduled, replaced with an agreed alternative, or credited. Any battery or accessory that raises operating weight to 250 g or more changes the applicable registration, certification and operating requirements.

## Floor plan and 360° instructions

Floor plans are for listing-marketing reference only. They are not surveys, architectural drawings, appraisals, construction plans or guaranteed measurements. The client must verify measurements and listing claims before publication.

A 360° virtual tour is an interactive hosted presentation, not a guaranteed measured 3D model. The final agreement should identify the hosting provider, delivery date, expiry date, renewal price, effect of expiry and reasonable limits for third-party outages or platform changes.

## Recommended cancellation starting point for counsel

Use this as a customer-friendly business position for the lawyer to convert into separate consumer and business clauses; do not paste it into every agreement without review.

- Client cancellation with at least 48 hours' notice: no cancellation fee.
- Client-requested rescheduling with 24–48 hours' notice: one change without an administrative fee, subject to availability.
- Weather, unsafe drone conditions, illness, emergency, or another condition outside reasonable control: reschedule without a cancellation penalty.
- Photographer cancellation or inability to provide the confirmed service: refund any amount that must legally be returned and offer a mutually acceptable reschedule; never promise a replacement photographer without the client's agreement.
- Business-client cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice, no-show, unavailable access, or a property that cannot reasonably be photographed: CA$75 only if that term is included in the accepted final agreement.
- Consumer cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice: charge only an amount permitted by the applicable agreement and mandatory law, such as reasonable documented work or non-recoverable expenses. Do not apply the business-client fee automatically.
- If actual non-recoverable costs may be charged, identify the categories in advance, require reasonable documentation, and distinguish consumer from business clients.
- Any cancellation policy must be shown before the final agreement is accepted, included in that agreement, and applied consistently.

## Final quote checklist

The final quote and agreement should contain editable fields for:

- Client legal name, mailing address, email and phone number.
- Client type, brokerage legal name and authority to arrange access and authorize the listing-media licence.
- Property address and project location.
- Exact package and each deliverable.
- Approved add-ons and their prices.
- Travel calculation, applicable taxes, total price, payment method and due dates.
- Shoot date, start window, expected duration and delivery targets.
- Revision allowance, RAW-file exclusion, archive period and 360 hosting dates.
- Named licence recipients and the current-listing-only licence term.
- Separate optional portfolio/social-media permission.
- Cancellation, rescheduling, unsafe-site, no-access, non-delivery and refund remedies.
- Contract language, governing law and legally required consumer disclosures.

## Items the lawyer must settle

- Fusco Media's legal contracting name, physical business address and signing capacity.
- Separate Quebec consumer, Ontario consumer and business-to-business versions or schedules.
- French-language contract and express English-language election process.
- Consumer-compliant cancellation, rescheduling, actual-cost and refund rules.
- Whether and how an advance payment may be collected for each client type and jurisdiction.
- Taxes, invoicing, travel calculation and treatment of approved extra work.
- Client and photographer cancellation, force majeure, non-delivery, reshoot and refund remedies.
- Liability limitations and all required exceptions for mandatory statutory rights.
- Privacy, data retention, service-provider disclosure and breach obligations.
- Electronic-signature evidence, document integrity, countersignature and delivery of a printable copy.
- Governing law, venue, notices, severability, amendments, assignment, subcontractors and authority to bind a property owner or brokerage.
